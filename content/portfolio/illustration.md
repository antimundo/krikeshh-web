## A Wedding at the Prado Museum
Personal project.
<p> An educational guide for teenagers. Two characters guide the visitor throughout the museum, the text includes fun facts about each of the paintings plus an activity to do at the end of the visit. Each side has a different theme for those who prefer "romantic paintings" or a bit more "dark and gore" themes.
